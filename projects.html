Projects

Selected computational and modeling directions. The descriptions emphasize methods and workflows rather than finalized claims.

Graphene Buckling Model

A mechanics model for studying periodic out-of-plane deformation in graphene-based sheets under compression. The workflow is intended to compare energy contributions, characteristic wavelengths, and boundary condition effects.

Haldane Model Wilson Loop / HWFC

Numerical routines for computing Wilson loops and hybrid Wannier function centers in Haldane-type lattice models. The goal is to track topological changes through robust, reproducible diagnostics.

LAMMPS Molecular Dynamics Simulation

Atomistic simulation workflows for relaxed structures and deformation processes in low-dimensional materials. These calculations support comparison between microscopic configurations and continuum mechanics assumptions.

ABAQUS Thin-Film Buckling Simulation

Finite-element models for thin-film buckling and wrinkling under controlled loading. The simulations are useful for testing continuum predictions and studying sensitivity to geometry and constraints.
